## 3.0.3 — 2026-10-19

- Every countdown keeps its own timer: as many as you like, on any key, profile, folder or deck, without sharing one.
- A countdown removed, or switched to another function, starts fresh when it is added again, instead of picking up the old timer on that key.
- Dragging a countdown to another key keeps its running timer (needs the current PyDeck).

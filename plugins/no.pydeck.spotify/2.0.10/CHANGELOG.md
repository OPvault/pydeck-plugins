## 2.0.10 — 2026-08-30

### Added

- A **Track Label Position** option on the Play / Pause face. The default
  keeps the layout as it was — time left along the top edge, the scrolling
  track label along the bottom — while **Top** swaps them, putting the song
  name at the top of the album art and the countdown at the bottom for
  anyone who reads the face that way round.
